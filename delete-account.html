CampusAlert Account & Data Deletion Request

Developer: Batangas State University – The National Engineering University (Lipa Campus)

App Name: CampusAlert

CampusAlert uses school Google Accounts (BatStateU institutional email) for sign-in and authentication. You can request the deletion of your CampusAlert user profile, account data, and associated records at any time using the methods below.

Method 1: In-App Deletion (Recommended)

If you currently have the CampusAlert app installed on your device:

  1. Open the CampusAlert application.
  2. Navigate to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete My Account.
  4. Confirm your selection.

Your authentication session and account credentials will be removed immediately upon completion.

Method 2: Web-Based Request (If App is Uninstalled)

If you have uninstalled the app or no longer have access to your device, you can request account deletion by emailing the Data Protection Officer or submitting an official request below:

Email Request: Send an email from your official BatStateU institutional Google account to:

To: dpo@g.batstate-u.edu.ph

Subject: CampusAlert Account Deletion Request - [Your Student ID]

Include: Full Name, Student ID Number, and a short statement requesting account deletion.

Data Retention & Deletion Timeline

  • Deleted Immediately: Authentication credentials, session tokens, and push notification tokens.
  • Deleted within 30 Days: Profile details (phone number, emergency contacts, college/section) and user-uploaded media (such as hazard photos).
  • Retained Data (Anonymized): Emergency incident reports, drill response logs, and audit trails are retained for institutional campus safety and compliance requirements under University records-retention policies. All personally identifying fields (such as name and email) are stripped from retained logs.

Contact Us

For questions regarding your data privacy rights under RA 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012), contact the University Data Protection Officer at dpo@g.batstate-u.edu.ph or visit the BatStateU Lipa Campus Administration Office.
