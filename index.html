CampusAlert Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 October 2026

CampusAlert is the emergency alert and campus safety application for Batangas State University – The National Engineering University (BatStateU-TNEU), Lipa Campus (“the University,” “we,” or “us”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, retain, and protect personal information when you use CampusAlert.

The University’s Data Protection Officer can be contacted at dpo@g.batstate-u.edu.ph or through the BatStateU Lipa Campus Administration Office.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use and the permissions you grant, CampusAlert may collect:

  • Account information: Your name, BatStateU Google account email address, Google profile photo, and student ID derived from your school email address.
  • Profile information you provide: Phone number, college, course, year, section, and up to three emergency contacts.
  • Location: With your permission, your last known location and related location details while you are on or near campus (approximately within 1 km). Away from campus, location may be collected when you choose to send an SOS, report your safety status, check in, or submit a medical report.
  • Emergency and safety information: Your status during alerts or drills, evacuation check-ins, SOS reports, and medical or hazard reports you submit. These reports may include notes, selected conditions, location, building, floor, and photos you deliberately take and submit.
  • Learning activity: Completed learning modules, quiz or game scores, experience points, and badges.
  • Device notification information: A notification token used to deliver alerts to your device via Firebase Cloud Messaging.

The app does not take or submit photos on its own. Photos are collected only when you choose to capture and submit one, such as with a hazard report.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Send real-time emergency alerts and safety notifications.
  • Account for students and staff during emergencies and safety drills.
  • Help authorized campus response personnel identify and contact people who may need immediate assistance.
  • Receive and respond to SOS, medical, hazard, and safety-status reports.
  • Provide preparedness lessons, quizzes, and related learning features.
  • Maintain and administer CampusAlert accounts and services.

3. Who May Receive Information

Information may be accessible to authorized BatStateU-TNEU Lipa Campus staff, faculty, and emergency response administrators who need it for emergency response, campus safety, account administration, or related University responsibilities.

CampusAlert uses third-party service providers:

  • Google Firebase: For authentication, push notification delivery, and secure database/storage of app information.
  • Google Sign-In: For institutional account authentication.
  • Cloudflare and Google Gemini: To generate automated responses to emergency queries submitted to the AI Disaster Guide. Your name and account credentials are not sent with those queries. Do not include personal or sensitive information in AI prompts.
  • OpenStreetMap and CARTO: For map tile rendering and geographic visual display.

These providers process information under their own privacy terms and the University’s arrangements with them. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

4. Location and Permissions

You can control location and notification permissions through your device settings and within CampusAlert settings. If you disable location access or location sharing, some safety and accountability features may not work as intended. You can also withdraw consent from your profile; this stops future collection covered by that consent and signs you out, but does not by itself delete information already collected.

Live location sharing: While location sharing is enabled and you are on or near campus, CampusAlert may periodically collect your precise location in the background and make your last reported location available to authorized campus administrators, including when you have not sent an SOS. This is strictly used to verify student safety and coordinate rescue operations within campus boundaries during emergencies and scheduled safety drills. You can turn off location sharing in Profile → Location Sharing Toggle. Location updates stop when sharing is off or the app no longer has the required device permission.

5. Data Retention

We retain account and profile information for as long as needed to provide and administer CampusAlert services during your active enrollment or employment at the University. Emergency, incident, drill, delivery, and audit records are retained in accordance with applicable University records-retention policies and safety reporting compliance requirements, even after an account deletion request. Where appropriate, personally identifying fields are removed from retained safety logs.

6. Account Deletion and Data Rights

To request account deletion, go to Profile → Delete My Account in CampusAlert. Your sign-in credentials and authentication records will be deleted immediately upon request completion. Associated profiles and user-submitted media (such as hazard photos) are queued for removal within 30 calendar days, while safety logs are anonymized.

To request access, correction, or deletion of specific data without closing your account, contact the Data Protection Officer. Withdrawing consent stops future data collection but does not alter previously processed emergency records.

7. Security and Data Transmission

The University and its cloud service providers employ administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to protect your personal information. Connections to cloud services (Firebase, Google Sign-In, and API services) are encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).

8. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, including the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), you have the right to be informed, access, correct, object to processing, request erasure or blocking of your personal data, and seek redress. To exercise these rights or raise a privacy concern, contact the Data Protection Officer or the National Privacy Commission of the Philippines.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when CampusAlert features or data practices change. We will publish the revised policy with an updated effective date on our hosting platform and notify users within the app.
